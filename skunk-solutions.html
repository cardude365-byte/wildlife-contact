Skunk Solutions

Skunks often take shelter under decks, porches, sheds and other low structures. Wildlife Ops focuses on resolving the immediate activity while also looking at the conditions that made the location attractive.

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Persistent skunk odor around the property
Burrowing under decks, porches, sheds or foundations
Tracks or disturbed soil around structures
Nighttime activity near lawns, landscaping or garbage
Pets repeatedly reacting to one area of the yard

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Identify the active area and determine how the skunk is using the property.

2

Use an appropriate control or removal strategy for the specific situation.

3

4

Prevention

Review food, shelter and landscape conditions that can encourage repeat activity.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
