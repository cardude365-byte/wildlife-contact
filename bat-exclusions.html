Professional Bat Control

Bat Exclusions

Bats in an attic, soffit, roofline or wall cavity need more than a quick patch. Wildlife Ops focuses on identifying active entry points, allowing bats to leave appropriately, and correcting the openings they use.

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Bats seen entering or leaving near the roofline at dusk
Droppings below an opening or in attic areas
Scratching or fluttering sounds in walls or ceilings
Dark staining around gaps, vents, fascia or soffits
A bat found inside the living space

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Inspection

Identify active entry points, secondary gaps and evidence of bat activity.

2

Exclusion Setup

Use appropriate one-way exclusion methods at active openings.

3

Seal & Secure

Correct accessible entry points after bats have had the opportunity to leave.

4

Prevention

Review vulnerable construction details and recommend additional work where needed.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
