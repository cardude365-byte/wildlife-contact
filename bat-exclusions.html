Common signs of bats

  • Bats seen entering or leaving near the roofline at dusk
  • Droppings below an entry point or in attic spaces
  • Scratching, fluttering or movement in walls and ceilings
  • Dark staining around gaps, vents, fascia or soffits
  • A bat found inside the living space

Why bats get into homes

Bats can use surprisingly small construction gaps. Roof intersections, ridge vents, fascia, soffits, flashing and other openings can become access points into attic and wall spaces.

Roof & fascia gaps

Small openings where rooflines meet trim or fascia can provide access to protected attic spaces.

Vents & soffits

Loose screening, damaged vents and construction gaps can become repeat entry points if they are not corrected.

Chimneys & transitions

Complex roof areas and transitions around chimneys or additions are common places to inspect carefully.

Our bat exclusion process

The goal is to remove access without trapping bats inside the structure, then correct the openings that allowed the problem to develop.

Inspection

We inspect the structure and look for active entry points, secondary gaps and evidence of bat activity.

Exclusion setup

At active openings, one-way exclusion devices or methods are installed so bats can leave without freely returning.

Seal & secure

After the exclusion phase, accessible entry points are sealed or repaired as appropriate for the structure.

Prevention

We review vulnerable areas and recommend additional repairs or cleanup when they are warranted.

Important: Bat work can be affected by season, weather, the location of a colony and applicable wildlife rules. Wildlife Ops evaluates the structure first and recommends the proper timing and method for the situation.

More than just getting the bats out

A good exclusion job focuses on the building itself. Simply removing a bat from a room does not solve an attic or structural entry problem.

Entry-point correction

We focus on where wildlife is actually entering and the nearby areas most likely to become future access points.

Cleanup options

When contamination or damaged insulation is present, cleanup and sanitation options can be discussed after inspection.
