Raccoon Problems

Raccoon Solutions

Raccoons can damage rooflines, soffits, vents and attic spaces while looking for shelter. Wildlife Ops evaluates where the animal is getting access and builds the response around the specific structure and activity.

Call 330-565-9322Text Wildlife Ops

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Heavy movement or thumping in an attic
Damaged soffit, fascia, vents or roof edges
Raccoon tracks, droppings or disturbed insulation
Activity around chimneys, decks or crawl areas
Repeated nighttime visits around the structure

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Inspect

Determine where activity is occurring and how raccoons are accessing the property.

2

Resolve the Activity

Use the appropriate control or removal approach for the situation.

3

Correct Access

Address vulnerable entry areas when exclusion or repair work is needed.

4

Reduce Repeat Problems

Recommend practical steps to make the property less attractive or accessible.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
