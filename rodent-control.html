Mice & Rats

Rodent Control

Rodent problems are rarely just about the animal you happen to see. Wildlife Ops looks at activity, entry points, food and shelter conditions so the control plan addresses why rodents are able to stay.

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Droppings in basements, garages, kitchens or utility areas
Scratching sounds inside walls or ceilings
Gnaw marks on stored items, wiring or building materials
Tracks, rub marks or repeated activity along edges
Gaps around foundations, doors, utilities or vents

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Inspection

Identify activity areas, likely entry points and conditions supporting the problem.

2

Control Plan

Use the appropriate trapping or baiting strategy for the site.

3

Entry Correction

Address accessible openings that allow rodents into the structure.

4

Monitoring

Track activity and adjust the program as needed until the problem is under control.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
