Wildlife Exclusion & Prevention

Home Protection

A long-term wildlife solution often comes down to the building itself. Wildlife Ops helps identify vulnerable areas and recommends practical exclusion and repair work designed to reduce future wildlife entry.

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Openings around soffits, fascia, vents or roof intersections
Damaged screening or loose construction materials
Recurring wildlife activity in the same part of the home
Gaps around utility penetrations or exterior transitions
Evidence that previous patching did not solve the source

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Inspect

Review the structure for active and potential wildlife entry points.

2

Prioritize

Separate active problem areas from locations that are simply vulnerable.

3

Exclude & Repair

Correct accessible problem areas with materials appropriate to the structure.

4

Maintain

Review future maintenance needs and areas worth watching over time.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
