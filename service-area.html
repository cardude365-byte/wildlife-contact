Service Area

Serving the Mahoning Valley and surrounding communities.

Wildlife Ops is based around the Canfield area and serves homeowners throughout the Mahoning Valley and nearby parts of Northeast Ohio. Travel range depends on the type of wildlife work involved.

Core Service Area

Trapping & animal-control work

Trapping jobs are generally kept closer to the core service area because active trapping can require repeated visits and regular trap checks.

As a general planning range, Wildlife Ops focuses trapping work within roughly 30 miles of the Canfield area.

Extended Travel

Exclusion & structural work

Bat exclusions, squirrel or raccoon exclusion, entry-point correction and other structural wildlife work may be available farther from the core route.

For the right project, travel of roughly 45–60 minutes may be practical depending on the scope and schedule.

How the range works

The job type matters.

A trapping route and a one-time exclusion inspection are very different operationally. That is why Wildlife Ops uses a tighter service radius for trapping while allowing more flexibility for structural exclusion work.

Outside the core area? Send your city or ZIP code and a quick description of the wildlife problem. If the job makes sense for the schedule and service type, Wildlife Ops can let you know what is available.
