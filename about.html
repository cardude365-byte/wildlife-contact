The Wildlife Ops Approach

Built around solving the whole problem.

Wildlife situations are rarely identical from one house to the next. The building, species, entry point, time of year and property conditions all matter.

Wildlife Ops starts with the actual evidence on the property: where the activity is occurring, what species is involved, how the animal is using the structure or landscape, and what conditions are allowing the problem to continue.

From there, the recommendation can include trapping or control, exclusion work, entry-point correction, cleanup or a combination of services. The point is to recommend what fits the situation rather than forcing every customer into the same package.

Homeowners should understand what was found and why a particular solution is being recommended. Clear explanations are part of the service.

What you can expect

  • A practical inspection of the problem area
  • A clear explanation of what was found
  • Options that fit the wildlife issue and property
  • Attention to entry points and repeat activity
  • Direct communication with Wildlife Ops
  • No unnecessary scare tactics or pressure
