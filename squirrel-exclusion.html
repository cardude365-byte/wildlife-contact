Squirrel Problems

Squirrel Exclusion

Squirrels commonly use roof edges, soffits, fascia and attic openings. The goal is to solve the current activity and correct the access points that allow squirrels to keep getting back inside.

Common Signs

These are some of the things homeowners commonly notice when this type of wildlife problem is developing.

Fast scratching or running sounds during daylight hours
Chewed or damaged fascia, soffit or vents
Nesting material or debris in attic areas
Squirrels repeatedly traveling across the same roof section
Visible gaps near eaves, dormers or roof intersections

How Wildlife Ops Approaches It

The exact plan depends on the species, the structure and what we find during the inspection.

1

Inspection

Locate active squirrel access points and inspect nearby vulnerable areas.

2

Control

Resolve the active animal problem using the method appropriate to the situation.

3

Exclusion

Close or reinforce access points when the structure is ready to be secured.

4

Follow-Up

Confirm the problem is resolved and review additional prevention needs.

Every property is different. Wildlife Ops evaluates the actual activity, entry points and structure before recommending the right control, exclusion or repair work.
